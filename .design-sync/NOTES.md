# Design sync notes

- The Design System («Consuelo C Design System») is plain HTML + CSS, maintained by hand: `components/<group>/<group>.css`, `components/<group>/*.card.html` (first line `<!-- @dsCard … -->`), `components/README.md`, `assets/icons.svg`. There is no React bundle and no converter run.
- The app (`src/lib/components/*.svelte`, `src/app.css`, `static/icons.svg`) is the source of truth. A sync is a targeted edit: read the remote file, apply the code's change to it, upload only the files that changed. Never a wholesale replace.
- The Design System keeps `lock` in its sprite although the app doesn't use it yet: planned by the navigation handoff (`uploads/ds-add-icons.md`).
- `wallet` (Caja's destination icon) is in both sprites since #191: the app's `static/icons.svg` took the Design System's path unchanged.
- `get_file` content only reaches the conversation, not the disk, so edited files are retyped before upload. Check `assets/icons.svg` symbols against `static/icons.svg` before uploading: a retyped path can carry a silent typo.
- The Hint (ⓘ tooltip, `.hint` / `.hint__text`) has no Svelte component: its styles are page-scoped in `src/routes/(app)/horas/equipo/+page.svelte`, and the Design System keeps them in `components/feedback/feedback.css`. Extract a component when a second screen uses it.
- Before uploading a retyped file, diff it against the same file in the latest Design Project export (`../design/_ds/<folder>/`). An identical diff proves the retype; then apply the edits with a script, so the final diff shows only them.
- The export can lag the remote: on 18 Oct 2026 its `forms.css` and `components/README.md` missed edits the remote had. So always `get_file` the remote first. When the remote reads the same as the export (same line count and comment lines), copy the export instead of retyping. Otherwise retype the remote, and check that the diff against the export shows only the remote's newer edits.
- To check a card before uploading, copy the export to the scratchpad, lay the staged files over it, and screenshot the card headless: `"/Applications/Brave Browser.app/Contents/MacOS/Brave Browser" --headless=new --allow-file-access-from-files --window-size=400,540 --screenshot=out.png file://…/card.html` (no Chrome on this machine).
- The desktop kits (`ui_kits/escritorio/index.html`, `pedido.html`, `impreso.html`) predate the shell: their sidebars carry «Ajustes» in the foot and lack Configuración and Caja. Left as they are on 20 Oct 2026; bring them in line with `navigation.card.html` when a sync touches those kits.
- The cash receipt (`src/routes/(app)/caja/comprobante/+page@.svelte`) keeps its print styles page-scoped, as overrides of `.print-sheet` and `.print-head`. The Design System names the same look `print-sheet--quarter` (since 20 Oct 2026, with `.print-head__number` and `.print-amount`): a sync of that page edits that modifier's block in `print.css`.
- `.choice` (fieldset, `__legend`, `__hint`, `__options`) is in `forms.css` since 20 Oct 2026. In the app it's page-scoped in `caja/+page.svelte` (filters, around a `.chip-row`) and `caja/registrar/+page.svelte` (Tipo and Método, in `.choice__options`). Extract it to `app.css` or a component when a third screen uses it.
- After the 20 Oct 2026 sync, `../design/_ds/` lags the remote on `components/README.md`, `print/print.css`, `print/cash-receipt.card.html`, `forms/forms.css` and `forms/forms.card.html` until the Design Project is re-exported.
- `navigation.card.html` is 740px wide since 21 Oct 2026, so its demo column is a 360px phone: three tabs (Sistema's) and Caja's tabs beside the filter only fit from there.
- After the 21 Oct 2026 sync (#212, menus), `../design/_ds/` lags the remote on `readme.md`, `components/README.md`, `navigation/navigation.css` and `navigation/navigation.card.html` until the Design Project is re-exported.
